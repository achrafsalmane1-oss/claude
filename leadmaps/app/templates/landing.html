Questions people ask before signing up

What counts as a lead?

One business row delivered to you. If a search returns 180 businesses, that is 180 leads off your monthly allowance. Failed searches are never charged.

Do you have my data locked in?

No. Every result set exports to CSV, and the API returns the same rows as JSON. Cancel and take your lists with you.

Is scraping public business listings legal?

You are responsible for how you use the data, including compliance with GDPR, CAN-SPAM and local marketing rules. See our terms.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from the billing page, any time. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
