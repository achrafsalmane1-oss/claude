Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

Colddrops ("we", "us") provides software for sending personalized voicemail messages. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. We wrote it to be readable, not to hide behind jargon.

What we collect

  • Account data — your email, hashed password, and workspace details.
  • Contact data you provide — the names, companies, phone numbers and emails you upload or source through the app, used solely to run your campaigns.
  • Campaign data — scripts, generated audio, delivery and callback status.
  • Usage data — basic logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

To provide the service: render voice messages, deliver drops, track results, meter your plan, and support you. We do not sell your contact lists. We use vetted sub-processors (voice, telephony/delivery, data enrichment, payments) only to perform these functions.

Your controls

  • Export your campaign contacts as CSV at any time.
  • Request deletion of your workspace and its data by contacting us.
  • People you contact can opt out; those numbers are suppressed permanently (see our DNC policy).
You are the controller of the contact data you bring to Colddrops. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to contact those people. We are your processor for that data.

Security

Passwords are salted and hashed. API keys are stored as server environment secrets, never in client code. Access to production data is limited to what's needed to run the service.

Contact

Questions or data requests: privacy@colddrops.app.
