The thesis

Cold calling is
the enemy.

Every ringing phone is an interruption no one asked for. The world is done being ambushed mid-sentence. The future of outbound isn't louder — it's invited. Haven trades the interruption for a message that waits patiently in the inbox, opened when your prospect actually has a moment to care.

How it works

Three quiet steps to a reply.

Write once, personalize infinitely, and let every message land in the right voice — automatically.

Step 01

Write your script

Drop in your message and sprinkle in variables. Haven merges each contact's details so every drop sounds handwritten — because, functionally, it is.

Step 02

Choose a voice that sounds human

Pick from studio-grade natural voices, or clone your own in thirty seconds. No robotic monotone — real warmth, real pauses, real you.

haven · script · Q3 founder outreach
Hey {{first_name}}, it's Sarah from Haven — I saw what you're building at {{company}} and had to reach out. No need to call me back, just wanted this waiting whenever you've got a sec. Talk soon.
Insert variable {{first_name}} {{company}} {{city}} {{title}}
Step 03

Drop it — no ring, ever

Haven deposits the message directly into voicemail. Their phone never rings. They see a notification, hear a real voice, and reply on their own terms.

Step 04

Watch the replies come in

Delivery, listens, and callbacks sync to your CRM in real time. Route hot leads, trigger follow-ups, and measure every drop.
