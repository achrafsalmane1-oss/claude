DNC & Acceptable Use

Last updated July 2026

Colddrops is built for permission-based outbound. This policy is mandatory and part of our Terms.

Your compliance obligations

  • Only contact people you have a lawful basis to reach.
  • Scrub your lists against applicable do-not-call / do-not-contact registries before sending.
  • Identify yourself truthfully in every message and provide a clear way to opt out.
  • Honor opt-outs immediately and permanently.

How Colddrops helps

  • Suppression list — every workspace has a do-not-contact list. Any number on it is skipped at send time, automatically, forever.
  • Send windows — drops are scheduled within reasonable local-time windows.
  • Audit trail — every drop, listen, and callback is logged.
Add a number to your suppression list from Settings, or via POST /api/suppressions. Opt-out requests captured from replies are suppressed automatically.

Opt out of being contacted

If you received a message via Colddrops and want to stop, reply to the sender to opt out, or email optout@colddrops.app with the number. We will suppress it across the sending workspace.

Prohibited uses

No contacting emergency lines; no harassment, deception, or impersonation; no use prohibited by law, regulation, or carrier policy. Violations can result in immediate suspension.
